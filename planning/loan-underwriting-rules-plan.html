Business app · 05 / 10 · Planning · ЯРЭ / Portal

Loan underwriting decision rules

Capture credit policy: applicant attributes, collateral links, limit rules, and mandatory hard stops as a conceptual schema with checkable statements.

Business purpose

Unify underwriting policy across channels; every auto-decision cites T#/P# from the published КС.

Actors: Credit risk, underwriters, compliance, product.

Why ЯРЭ + Concept Portal

  • Hard stops and soft conditions separate cleanly into axioms/predicates vs scoring outside schema.
  • Small КМ fixtures encode known accept/reject cases for regression.
  • Portal versioning supports regulator-facing change logs.

Conceptual core (sketch)

Неопределяемые понятия

AliasRole / convention sketch
X1Applicants
X2Loan products
X3Collateral items
S1ℬ(X1×X2×Z×Z) — application: product, amount, term
S2ℬ(X1×X3) — applicant pledges collateral
S3ℬ(X2×Z×Z) — product min/max amount
S4ℬ(X1) — applicants failing KYC hard stop (as set)

Производные / проверки

AliasIntent
P1Amount within product bounds
P2Collateral required products have non-empty pledge
P3Applicant not in KYC fail set
T1Auto-eligible ⇔ P1 ∧ P2 ∧ P3
F1Required checks list for a product

Aliases are a starting sketch for conceptualization — refine with BASE-SELECTION and avoid new X#/C#/S# when a D#/F#/P# suffices.

Concept Portal usage

  • КС Biz/UnderwritingCore; product catalog synthesized from Biz/LoanProducts.
  • Keep scoring models outside ЯРЭ; schema owns hard eligibility only.
  • Compliance reads Portal; agents validate diagnostics before release.

rstool-helper delivery workflow

  1. Split hard vs soft policy with risk owners.
  2. Encode only hard stops in A#/P#/T#.
  3. Fixture КМ from last 10 policy exceptions.
  4. Publish; map LOS decision engine to T1.
