Project portfolio dependency & capacity

Represent initiative dependencies, shared capability demands, and conflict predicates so PMO can see infeasible portfolios before funding.

Business purpose

Fund only portfolios whose dependency graph and capacity claims are coherent under a published conceptual schema.

Actors: PMO, capability owners, finance portfolio committee.

Why ЯРЭ + Concept Portal

  • Dependencies are relations; capacity claims are structured tuples with Z effort.
  • Conflict predicates catch double-booked critical capabilities.
  • Portal holds enterprise PMO schema; quarterly КМ per planning cycle.

Concept Portal usage

  • КС Biz/PortfolioFeasibility; capability catalog shared via synthesize.
  • Each planning round = КМ with S4 candidate set.
  • Committee reviews T1; agents propose minimal add/drop sets (outside pure ЯРЭ if search-heavy).

Risks & reductions

  • Full project scheduling — out of scope; schema checks funding/capacity coherence only.
  • Hidden soft dependencies not in S1 — enforce intake quality.

Success criteria

Schema ready

No expression/schema diagnostics; conventions in business language; review checklist passed.

Operations adopted
