Inventory reservation & allocation rules

Decide how stock is reserved across orders and warehouses: priority, substitutable SKUs, and feasibility predicates before promising delivery.

Business purpose

ATP/allocation policy becomes a shared conceptual definition for OMS, WMS, and sales promise dates.

Actors: Supply chain planner, OMS owner, warehouse manager.

Why ЯРЭ + Concept Portal

  • Allocation feasibility is set-theoretic over stock and demand relations.
  • Substitutions are explicit S#; derived available-to-promise as D#/F#.
  • Portal schema reviewed by supply chain; models hold snapshot stock.

Concept Portal usage

  • КС Biz/AllocationPolicy; stock snapshots as КМ per planning run.
  • Do not put live stock into schema text; only relations/rules.
  • Agents run evaluate on planning KM exports.

rstool-helper delivery workflow

  1. Reduce: satisfiability + priority, not full LP solver.
  2. If full optimization out of scope, schema only checks feasibility predicates.
  3. КМ with scarce stock; verify priority T1 narrative with explicit allocation S input.
  4. Portal publish for planners.
